Contributed to the in silico design of small-molecule ligands to inhibit early-stage nucleation of insulin aggregation. Identified EGCG and polyoxometalates as candidate ligands through literature review and computational modeling, and docked EGCG into a partially folded insulin intermediate using ROSIE, the Rosetta modeling web server.
