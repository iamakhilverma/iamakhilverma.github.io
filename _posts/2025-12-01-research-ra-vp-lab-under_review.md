We called mutations in 15.5 million SARS-CoV-2 genomes from GISAID (5.6 million after quality filtering, December 2019 to December 2025) and found that early recurrence predicts which mutations become predominant: 79–85% of early mutations that later reached ≥1% frequency had recurred within 28 days of first detection.
